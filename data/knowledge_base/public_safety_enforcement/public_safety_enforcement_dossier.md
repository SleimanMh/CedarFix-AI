# Public Safety and Enforcement Dossier

## Routing Summary

- Civil Defense is first for active fire, explosion, gas, rescue, entrapment, active collapse, and immediate life-safety danger.
- ISF is first for crime, weapon, threat, public order, and traffic accident control; Civil Defense is secondary when rescue or injury is present.
- Municipalities and municipal police handle non-emergency local enforcement: parking, noise, sidewalk/public-space obstruction, illegal construction, dangerous trees/poles, and stray animals, with HITL when danger or asset ownership is unclear.
- Central Inspection handles corruption, misconduct, negligence, and complaints against public administrations; DGLAC handles local-administration registration/form tracking.

## Evidence Position

The shard uses registered source IDs for Civil Defense 125, ISF mission/units/FAQ, municipal governance references, Central Inspection mandate/contact/complaint process, DGLAC contact/FAQ, and MOIM municipal-governance context.

## Known Limits

- Municipal police contact coverage is dynamic and mostly municipality-specific.
- ISF station/traffic-unit geographies are not yet mapped.
- No universal public SLA for municipal enforcement is encoded.
