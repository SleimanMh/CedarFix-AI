## Important Notes for Reviewers

- All `miye` forms in this script are **wrong** — use `may` (standard Lebanese)
- `sa32` is kept as HIGH_RISK phrase component only; primary shock word is `shar2ata`
- `5atar`, `5atr`, `5tr` are in `HIGH_RISK_HINTS` — promote with care
- `masalla7`, `mshbouh`, `sa32` are in `HIGH_RISK_HINTS`
- Terms with `false_friend_risk=true` in the bank need extra care:
  - `7ara` = lane OR neighbourhood
  - `darb` = road OR hitting
  - `beer` = well (beer may) — not the drink
  - `3ain` = spring OR eye OR surveillance

See `scripts/validate_arabizi_candidate_bank.py` for full validation rules.
